Frequently asked questions

How do I order products?

Add items to your cart and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. Your order arrives as a message — we confirm price, stock and delivery time right away.

Do you deliver at home?

Yes, we deliver across Multan. Share your address in the cart and we'll arrange delivery.

Are your products original?

Yes — original, fresh stock with long expiry dates, from both local and imported brands.

Do I need an appointment for the clinic?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking through the form means less waiting — especially for vaccinations and grooming.

Can I leave my pet for boarding?

Yes, boarding is available at our Gulgasht branch. Book early around Eid and holidays.

Do you offer wholesale rates for shops?

Yes — our Saddar Cantt branch is Multan's first wholesale pet shop. WhatsApp 0308-6351786 for bulk rates.

Run a pet shop or clinic?
