Questions

Good to know before you visit

Can't find your answer? Message us — we usually reply quickly on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
Do you deliver pet food at home?

Yes. Send your order on WhatsApp with your address and we'll arrange home delivery in Multan.

Do I need an appointment for the clinic?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead through the form above means less waiting — especially for vaccinations and grooming.

Which pets do you treat?

Mainly cats and dogs, along with birds and other small pets. Tell us about your pet when you book and we'll confirm.

Do you have imported food brands?

We stock both local and imported food, treats and litter, plus accessories like bowls, carriers, travel baskets, chains and cat houses.

Can I leave my pet for boarding while I travel?

Yes — boarding is available at our Gulgasht branch. Book early around Eid and holidays, as spaces fill up fast.
